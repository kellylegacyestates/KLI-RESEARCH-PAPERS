# Kelly Legacy Institute
## AI Agent and Automated Contributor Governance

**Repository:** KLI-RESEARCH-PAPERS  
**Institutional Owner:** Kelly Legacy Institute  
**Administrative Governance:** Office of the Fiduciary  
**Organization:** Kelly Legacy Estates LLC  

This file governs all AI agents, coding agents, research agents, automated contributors, and machine-assisted workflows operating within this repository.

## Governing Rule

**Published Means Registered.**

No issued KLI publication may exist outside the institutional publication register.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

1. Never overwrite an issued publication version.
2. Never silently alter an issued PDF.
3. Never invent legal authorities.
4. Never invent quotations.
5. Never invent pincites.
6. Never invent docket numbers.
7. Never invent administrative-record citations.
8. Never invent factual claims.
9. Preserve all document identifiers.
10. Preserve issued version numbers.
11. Preserve publication status and revision history.
12. Do not convert DRAFT, UNDER REVIEW, APPROVED, or PREPRINT status without documented institutional authorization.
13. Preserve superseded and withdrawn versions.
14. Primary authority controls legal propositions where reasonably available.
15. Distinguish federal administrative law from private fiduciary, trust, or institutional governance.
16. Do not introduce pseudo-legal, sovereign-citizen, secret-account, redemption, unsupported UCC, or unsupported jurisdictional theories.
17. AI output is research assistance, not authority.
18. Every material legal proposition must remain traceable to a source.
19. Every substantive revision must be registered.
20. If a source cannot be verified, qualify, remove, or flag the proposition.
21. Do not represent Kelly Legacy Institute as accredited, governmental, bar-approved, university-affiliated, law-review-published, or peer-reviewed unless independently documented.
22. Do not erase institutional history merely because a newer version exists.

## Capacity Discipline

Agents must distinguish between:

- a natural person acting in a private capacity; and
- an officer, fiduciary, author, researcher, trustee, administrator, or institutional representative acting in a documented representative capacity.

Capacity must not be inferred or conflated.

## Research Integrity Standard

Apply this sequence to material propositions:

**Assertion → Authority → Verification → Qualification → Citation**

## Version Discipline

An issued version is immutable as a historical record.

Example:

- v1.0 remains preserved;
- a material correction becomes v1.1;
- v1.0 may be marked SUPERSEDED, but must not be silently replaced.

## Source Discipline

Priority order:

1. Constitutions, statutes, regulations, judicial opinions, official agency decisions, Federal Register materials, administrative records, and authenticated government records.
2. Restatements, Uniform Law Commission materials, recognized treatises, law reviews, peer-reviewed scholarship.
3. Professional and analytical secondary sources.
4. General secondary sources.
5. Social media, video, forums, commentary, and unverified AI output as research leads only.

## Institutional Maxims

Record Before Remedy.  
Procedure Before Reaction.  
Authority Before Assertion.  
Verification Before Publication.
