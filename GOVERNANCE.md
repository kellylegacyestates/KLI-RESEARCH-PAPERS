# Kelly Legacy Institute
## Repository Governance Standard

**Repository:** KLI-RESEARCH-PAPERS  
**Institutional Owner:** Kelly Legacy Institute  
**Administrative Governance:** Office of the Fiduciary  
**Organization:** Kelly Legacy Estates LLC  

## 1. Purpose

This repository is the canonical institutional repository for the Kelly Legacy Institute Research Paper Series.

It preserves issued research papers, scholarly preprints, verification memoranda, source registers, version histories, corrections, superseded records, withdrawn records, publication standards, and research-governance controls.

## 2. Canonical Record

The repository record controls the institutional publication history of the KLI Research Paper Series.

Public copies may also appear on websites, scholarly platforms, or other distribution channels, but those copies do not replace the controlled repository record.

## 3. Governing Rule

**Published Means Registered.**

Every issued publication must be entered into the Publication Register.

## 4. Publication States

Approved status values:

- DRAFT
- UNDER REVIEW
- APPROVED
- PREPRINT
- PUBLISHED
- SUPERSEDED
- WITHDRAWN
- ARCHIVED

## 5. Version Control

An issued version is immutable as a historical record.

A substantive correction receives a new version number.

Example:

v1.0 → v1.1

The earlier version may be marked SUPERSEDED but shall remain preserved unless removal is required by law, privacy, security, or another documented institutional obligation.

## 6. Corrections

Material errors shall be recorded, reviewed, corrected through a new version or correction notice, entered into the Corrections Register, and preserved in the institutional history.

Silent substantive correction is prohibited.

## 7. Supersession

A later version may supersede an earlier version.

Superseded records remain preserved for institutional history and citation integrity.

## 8. Withdrawal

A publication may be withdrawn where material error, confidentiality, privacy, security, legal obligation, or institutional review makes continued reliance inappropriate.

Withdrawal must be documented.

## 9. Research Verification

Material legal propositions must follow:

**Assertion → Authority → Verification → Qualification → Citation**

Primary authority should be used where reasonably available.

Contrary authority must not be omitted merely because it weakens a preferred conclusion.

## 10. Source Hierarchy

### Tier I
Primary authority and official records.

### Tier II
Restatements, Uniform Law Commission materials, recognized treatises, law reviews, and peer-reviewed scholarship.

### Tier III
Professional and analytical sources.

### Tier IV
General secondary sources.

### Tier V
Research leads only, including social media, commentary, forums, and unverified AI output.

## 11. Agent Governance

All automated contributors must comply with AGENTS.md.

AI output is research assistance, not authority.

## 12. Capacity Discipline

The repository must distinguish private individual capacity from representative, fiduciary, institutional, authorial, or administrative capacity.

## 13. Archive Integrity

Institutional history shall not be erased merely because a publication is corrected, superseded, or withdrawn.

## 14. Public Access

Public availability does not convert this repository or Kelly Legacy Institute into a law firm, governmental body, court, university, law school, or accredited academic institution.

## 15. Amendment

Changes to this governance standard must be documented through version history and institutional review.

## Institutional Maxims

Record Before Remedy.  
Procedure Before Reaction.  
Authority Before Assertion.  
Verification Before Publication.
